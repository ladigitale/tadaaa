# Pricing

Tadaaa is **free**. There is no paid plan, no price and no cost to use the hosted demo at app.tadaaa.space; the software is open source (MIT) and free to self-host.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Local only (no account) | Free — 0 € | Tasks and tags stored in your browser, offline, no server |
| Free cloud account | Free — 0 € | Cloud sync, sharing, webhooks, embeds, MCP access, within the quotas below |
| Self-hosted | Free (MIT) — you pay your own hosting | Your own quotas and domain |

## Free tier quotas

The free cloud account has a storage quota and daily / monthly bandwidth quotas set by the operator. Your current limits and usage are shown in the app under **Connectivity → Usage**. Contact an admin if you need more.
