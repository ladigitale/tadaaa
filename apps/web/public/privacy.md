# Privacy policy

This policy describes how personal data is processed when you use a Tadaaa cloud account on app.tadaaa.space. The full, up-to-date version with the data controller's details is at https://app.tadaaa.space/legal/privacy.

- **Local-only use**: without an account, tasks stay in your browser (IndexedDB) and are not sent to the server.
- **Purposes**: create and manage your account; store and sync tasks, tags and datasets; optional push notifications; sharing and invites; webhooks, embeds and MCP access tokens; security and abuse prevention (including limited audit / usage metrics).
- **Legal bases**: performance of the contract (account and sync), legitimate interest (security, service improvement), and consent where required (optional push notifications, acceptance of terms at sign-up).
- **Recipients**: the publisher and the hosting provider; technical processors required to run the service (email delivery, push gateways such as FCM / Mozilla, optional PeerJS for P2P sharing). Data is not sold.
- **Retention**: account and cloud data while your account is active, deleted when you delete your account (or when an admin does). Technical logs may be kept for a limited period for security.
- **Cookies**: no advertising or analytics cookies; session and preferences use browser storage.
- **Your rights (GDPR)**: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. In the app you can export your data and delete your account from Account settings.
- **Contact**: app@tadaaa.space. You may also lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (e.g. CNIL in France).
